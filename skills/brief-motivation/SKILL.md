# Brief Motivation

Use this skill to rank the five psychology drivers for the target experience: progression, status, FOMO, mastery, and community.

## Output

Return a **Motivation Matrix** with:
- ranked drivers,
- why each driver fits or does not fit,
- anti-patterns and overuse risks,
- Roblox-fit notes.

## Rules

- Explain the dominant driver in one sentence.
- Separate “good urgency” from manipulative pressure.
- Prefer community when social play is naturally supported.
